Can

Present
Meaning
Past

Ability

Informal
Can you tell us about yourself and your art?
Request
Formal
Could you please tell us about some of your most memorable drawings?
Be able to
Be able to has the same meaning as can, but you can use it in Present, Past or Future.
Present
He is able to draw unique paintings.
Past
I wasn't able to draw anything nice.
Future
I will be able to visit more places.
