I will never forget my trip to Italy in 2000. I was on a boat on the Adriatic Sea. There were tourists from different countries, too. A teenage boy with his father sat in front of me. The captain stopped the boat after 35 minutes in the middle of the sea. It was warm and the view was beautiful! We all stood up to look around. The teenager wanted to look at the fish and went closer to the water. Guess what? He fell into the water! We were all shocked and didn't know what to do! Suddenly, we saw two big dolphins in the sea. The dolphins noticed the boy in the sea and swam to help! One of them lifted the boy and threw him into the boat! That was incredible! The dolphin rescued the boy! His father was very grateful to his son's rescuer! I will remember this story forever!
