Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart is a famous Austrian composer. He was born in 1756 in Salzburg, Austria. He learned to play the piano when he was 3 years old. He also learned to play the violin and a year later the organ. He started composing music at the age of 5. His father took him to many European cities and Mozart gave concerts in Germany, France, England and Switzerland. People loved listening to his music and were excited to see young and talented Mozart. He was the author of many songs. Unfortunately, Mozart lived a very short life. He died in 1791, at the age of 35. People all over the world love his music and enjoy listening to it.
