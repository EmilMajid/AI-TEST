GRAMMAR TARGET
Zero Conditional
We use Zero Conditional to talk about facts and things which are generally true.
Example: If you don't water plants, they die. (fact)
When I need a true opinion, I always ask him. (This is the truth about my life.)

Note: Main clause can come at the beginning or after the If-clause.
