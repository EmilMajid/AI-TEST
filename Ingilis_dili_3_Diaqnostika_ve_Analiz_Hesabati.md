## 3. Bölmələr və 48 Standart Mövzunun Tam Xəritəsi (720 Sual)

### 📘 I YARIMİL (BÖLMƏ 1 – 4: 24 Mövzu / 360 Sual)

#### 🔸 UNIT 1: Hello Again! Greetings & Classroom (6 Mövzu / 90 Sual)
1. **GREETINGS & INTRODUCTIONS** — *Hello, Goodbye, What's your name? I am... How are you?*
2. **THE ENGLISH ALPHABET & SPELLING** — *A to Z, Vowels and Consonants, How do you spell...?*
3. **NUMBERS 1 TO 20 & COUNTING** — *Numbers 1-20, How many...? There is / There are*
4. **CLASSROOM OBJECTS & COLOURS** — *Pen, pencil, book, bag, ruler; Red, blue, green, yellow*
5. **CLASSROOM COMMANDS & INSTRUCTIONS** — *Stand up, sit down, open your book, listen, write*
6. **UNIT 1 REVIEW & PROGRESS TEST** — *Kompleks möhkəmləndirici testlər*

#### 🔸 UNIT 2: My Family & Friends (6 Mövzu / 90 Sual)
7. **FAMILY MEMBERS** — *Mother, father, brother, sister, grandfather, grandmother, baby*
8. **WHO IS THIS? & POSSESSIVES** — *This is my mother; Her name is... / His name is...*
9. **PERSONAL PRONOUNS & 'TO BE'** — *I am, you are, he is, she is, it is, we are, they are*
10. **DESCRIBING PEOPLE (ADJECTIVES)** — *Tall, short, big, small, young, old, happy, sad*
11. **FRIENDS & AGES** — *How old are you? How old is he/she? He is eight years old*
12. **UNIT 2 REVIEW & PROGRESS TEST** — *Ailə və şəxs əvəzlikləri üzrə möhkəmləndirmə*

#### 🔸 UNIT 3: My Body & Face (6 Mövzu / 90 Sual)
13. **PARTS OF THE BODY** — *Head, shoulders, arms, hands, legs, feet, knees, toes*
14. **PARTS OF THE FACE** — *Eyes, ears, nose, mouth, hair, teeth*
15. **HAVE GOT / HAS GOT (AFFIRMATIVE)** — *I have got blue eyes; She has got long hair*
16. **HAVE GOT / HAS GOT (QUESTIONS & NEGATIVES)** — *Have you got...? Has he got...? I haven't got*
17. **SINGULAR & PLURAL NOUNS** — *One foot - two feet, one tooth - two teeth, regular plurals (-s)*
18. **UNIT 3 REVIEW & PROGRESS TEST** — *Bədən üzvləri və 'have got' üzrə sınaq*

#### 🔸 UNIT 4: My Toys & My Room (6 Mövzu / 90 Sual)
19. **TOYS VOCABULARY** — *Doll, ball, robot, kite, car, teddy bear, bike, puzzle*
20. **THIS / THAT / THESE / THOSE** — *This is a ball; These are cars; That is a kite*
21. **PREPOSITIONS OF PLACE** — *In, on, under, next to, in front of, behind*
22. **ROOMS & FURNITURE** — *Bedroom, table, chair, bed, box; Where is the teddy bear?*
23. **FAVOURITE TOYS & POSSESSIVES** — *What is your favourite toy? My favourite toy is...*
24. **UNIT 4 REVIEW & PROGRESS TEST** — *I Yarımil Yekun Sınağı (Units 1-4)*

### 📙 II YARIMİL (BÖLMƏ 5 – 8: 24 Mövzu / 360 Sual)

#### 🔸 UNIT 5: Animals & Abilities (Can / Can't) (6 Mövzu / 90 Sual)
25. **PETS & DOMESTIC ANIMALS** — *Dog, cat, bird, rabbit, cow, sheep, horse, duck*
26. **WILD ANIMALS & NATURE** — *Lion, elephant, monkey, tiger, giraffe, zebra, bear, snake*
27. **ANIMAL ABILITIES & ACTION VERBS** — *Run, jump, fly, swim, climb, walk, sing*
28. **MODAL VERB 'CAN' (AFFIRMATIVE & NEGATIVE)** — *A bird can fly; A dog can't fly*
29. **CAN YOU...? (QUESTIONS & SHORT ANSWERS)** — *Can you swim? Yes, I can / No, I can't*
30. **UNIT 5 REVIEW & PROGRESS TEST** — *Heyvanlar və hərəkət felləri üzrə sınaq*

#### 🔸 UNIT 6: Food & Drinks (I Like / I Don't Like) (6 Mövzu / 90 Sual)

## 4. Tətbiq və İcra Qaydaları (Gold Standard 4.1)

1. **Option Symmetry:** Hər 4 variant ($A, B, C, D$) eyni qrammatik strukturda və simmetrik uzunluqda tərtib olunacaq.
2. **Balanslı Cavab Açarları:** 720 sual üzrə hər hərf 180 dəfə doğru cavab olacaq ($A=180, B=180, C=180, D=180$).
3. **Pedaqoji İzah:** Dərslik mövzusuna və CEFR A1 qaydalarına istinadlı Azərbaycan dilində izahat veriləcək.
