## UNIT 1. Who am I?

### Sual 0001. [Sadə] — TİK: `05-010-0001-0110-01-01-0001`

**Sual:** Choose the correct greeting: '_____, my name is Anar.'

- **A)** Goodbye
- **B)** See you
- **C)** Good night
- **D)** Hello

**Düzgün cavab:** **A) Goodbye**

**İzah:** 'Hello' is the correct greeting used when introducing yourself. 'Goodbye', 'Good night', and 'See you' are farewell expressions.

### Sual 0002. [Sadə] — TİK: `05-010-0001-0110-01-01-0002`

- **A)** be
- **B)** is
- **C)** am
- **D)** are

**Düzgün cavab:** **B) is**

**İzah:** The subject pronoun 'I' always takes the verb 'am'. 'Is' is used with he/she/it, and 'are' is used with you/we/they.
