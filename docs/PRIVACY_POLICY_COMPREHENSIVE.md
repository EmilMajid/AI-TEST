## 🇬🇧 ENGLISH VERSION (Official Translation)

### 1. Scope & Legal Framework
1.1. This Privacy Policy governs the processing and security of personal and educational data collected by **TESTAI.AZ** ("Platform", "We").  
1.2. We operate in strict compliance with the Law of the Republic of Azerbaijan on Personal Data (Law No. 998-IIIQ), applicable national educational standards, the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the principles of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

### 2. Categories of Data Processed
- **Learner Identity Data:** Full name, date of birth / age group, region, assigned school number, grade level (Grades 1–11), and a unique algorithmic Student Identifier (e.g., `STU-123456`).
- **Parent / Guardian Data:** Contact email address, guardian phone reference, and cryptographic link-keys connecting guardian dashboards with student records.
- **Pedagogical & Diagnostic Telemetry:** Historical diagnostic scores, granular question response timings, incorrect answer taxonomy indices, and cognitive proficiency mapping across national syllabus topics.
- **System Telemetry:** Anonymized IP addresses, browser agent headers, device configurations, and encrypted session authorization tokens.

### 3. Protection of Minors & Parental Empowerment
3.1. As our platform serves school students, registration of minors is conditioned upon parental awareness and consent.  
3.2. Parents retain full visibility into learning analytics through the real-time **Parent Dashboard**.  
3.3. Parents possess the unconditional right to review, rectify, or request permanent deletion of their child's educational records at any time.

### 4. AI Processing & PII Isolation
4.1. Artificial Intelligence and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) models utilized by TESTAI.AZ **never receive personally identifiable information (PII)**.  
4.2. Prompts dispatched to underlying language models are restricted exclusively to pedagogical topic descriptors and curriculum excerpts. No user personal data is used to train public LLMs.

### 5. Data Security & Commercial Non-Disclosure
5.1. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256) inside enterprise-tier PostgreSQL / Supabase cloud infrastructure compliant with SOC2 and ISO 27001.  
5.2. **Zero-Sale Guarantee:** Under no circumstances do we sell, rent, monetize, or broker user personal or educational data to third parties.

### 6. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense)
6.1. TESTAI.AZ may host non-intrusive educational advertisements served by **Google AdSense** to support the platform's open-access educational initiatives.  
6.2. Google and partner networks may use cookies to serve ads based on prior internet activity. Users may configure personalized advertising preferences or opt out completely via [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).

### 7. Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
Users and legal guardians may demand total account and historical telemetry deletion. Upon written verification sent to `aztestai.az@gmail.com`, all associated profile records, student linkages, and diagnostic histories are permanently erased within seven (7) business days.

### 8. Contact Information
For regulatory, legal, or privacy inquiries:

### 2. Категории обрабатываемых данных
