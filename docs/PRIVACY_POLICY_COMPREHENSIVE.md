### 1. General Provisions & Legal Scope
1.1. This Privacy Policy sets forth the principles governing the collection, processing, storage, transfer, protection, and erasure of personal data of all users (including students, parents, legal guardians, and educators) of the **TESTAI.AZ** educational assessment platform ("TESTAI.AZ", "Platform", "We").

The United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA") is observed solely where applicable to operations involving children under 13 years of age.  
1.3. "Personal Data" refers to any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual. "Processing" includes collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation, retrieval, anonymization, transfer, and erasure under applicable statutes.

### 2. Data Controller & Processor
2.1. The official controller and operator of personal data on TESTAI.AZ:
- **Managing Entity:** TESTAI.AZ Educational Technologies Team
- **Official Inquiries:** aztestai.az@gmail.com
- **Platform URL:** https://testai.az  
2.2. Where third-party infrastructure providers are engaged for technical hosting or database storage, such entities process data strictly as service providers/processors under statutory safeguards.

### 3. Categories of Personal Data Processed
- **Student Data:** Full name; date of birth or age bracket; educational district/city; school identifier; grade level (Grades 1–11); unique algorithmic Student ID (e.g., STU-123456); credential tokens.
- **Parent & Guardian Data:** Full name (where provided); contact email; phone reference; cryptographic link-keys connecting student records with the Parent Dashboard; records of parental verification.
- **Pedagogical & Diagnostic Telemetry:** Assessment scores; date and duration of diagnostic sessions; specific incorrect and correct responses; topic mastery analytics; problem-solving duration metrics.
- **Technical Metrics:** IP addresses (anonymized or masked where technically implemented); browser and OS identifiers; authentication tokens (such as JWT); security and audit logs.

### 4. Purposes of Data Processing
Data is processed strictly for:
1. Account provisioning and secure authentication;
2. Connecting student and parental supervisory dashboards;
3. Rendering curriculum-aligned diagnostic assessments;
4. Formulating learning trajectory analytics delivered to the Parent Dashboard;
5. Ensuring platform security and mitigating fraudulent access;
6. Fulfilling statutory and educational regulatory obligations.

### 5. Legal Grounds for Processing
Processing is grounded in compliance with Articles 8 and 9 of the Law of the Republic of Azerbaijan on Personal Data. For minors lacking full legal capacity, consent is obtained from a parent or authorized legal guardian.

### 6. Protection of Children's Personal Data
6.1. As a platform serving school-aged learners, TESTAI.AZ exercises stringent diligence regarding minor privacy.  
6.2. In compliance with national laws, minor profiling and processing require parental knowledge and consent.  
6.3. Where COPPA applies, verifiable parental consent principles governing children under 13 are observed.  
6.4. Parents retain the right to review, update, or demand the erasure of their child's educational records at any time.

### 7. Artificial Intelligence & RAG Operations
7.1. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is utilized for question generation and diagnostic analytics.  
7.2. Prompts dispatched to underlying language models are technically isolated: direct identifying data (name, email, school) is omitted. Queries utilize solely topic descriptors, grade tier, and textbook syllabus context.  
7.3. User personal data is never used to train public language models.  
7.4. AI-generated outcomes provide diagnostic insight and do not replace official school instructor grading.

### 8. Third-Party Disclosures & Subprocessors
8.1. TESTAI.AZ does not sell, lease, monetize, or broker personal or educational data for commercial marketing.  
8.2. Data is processed exclusively through vetted enterprise infrastructure (Supabase/PostgreSQL, Vercel/Next.js hosting).  
8.3. According to official vendor documentation, Supabase cloud infrastructure adheres to SOC 2 Type 2 frameworks and holds ISO/IEC 27001 certification. This status does not constitute independent standalone certification of TESTAI.AZ.

### 9. Technical & Organizational Security

### 10. Cookie Technologies
